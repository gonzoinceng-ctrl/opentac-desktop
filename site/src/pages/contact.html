Contact

OpenTac is a small, unofficial project. Before writing, please check whether one of these is the better place.

  • Problems with the Mac app or its installer: open an issue at github.com/gonzoinceng-ctrl/opentac-desktop/issues. Others can find and add to it there.
  • Questions about the engine or the globe itself (layers, data, voice control, what a button does): these are best raised in the upstream God's Eye View repository. OpenTac does not change the engine, and the upstream author is the person who can answer.

Send a message

Messages are stored on Cloudflare, which hosts this site, and are read by hand. Nothing is emailed or sent anywhere else, and they are deleted automatically after 90 days. Replies are not guaranteed. See the privacy page.

Only used to reply to you.

10 to 5000 characters. Please do not include API keys or passwords.
