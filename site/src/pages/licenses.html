OpenTac and the software it bundles

OpenTac's own code is MIT-licensed. The app bundles or depends on:

  • Electron (MIT) and Chromium (BSD and other open-source licenses).
  • Node.js (MIT and others). The license file ships inside the app at Resources/node/LICENSE.
  • git, through dugite (GNU GPL v2). Shipped as a separate executable and never linked into OpenTac; the source is at github.com/git/git.
  • electron-updater, electron-log, tree-kill (MIT) and semver (ISC).

The complete list, with every license text, is in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the OpenTac repository. The same notices are available from the app's About panel.
